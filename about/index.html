About the publication

Medicine is human.
So are its stories.

The Tempest is a student-run literary and arts magazine of Mayo Clinic Alix School of Medicine.

Through poetry, prose, photography, and visual art, the publication makes room for the experiences that shape a life in medicine. Each annual issue brings these perspectives together in print, with a growing collection of companion works online.
