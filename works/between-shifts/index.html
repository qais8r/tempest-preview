Between shifts

by Jordan Kim

The coffee had gone cold by the time the sun reached the windows. Outside, someone was watering a row of small trees. Inside, the hallway was beginning its familiar rhythm.

I had spent the morning collecting facts. A temperature. A date. The name of a medication written twice, once carefully and once in a hurry. But what I remembered was a man asking whether the rain had stopped.

I went to the window to find out.
