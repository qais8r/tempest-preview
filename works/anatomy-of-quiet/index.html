Poetry · 2026

Anatomy of quiet

by Alex Morgan

The companion

A closer look at
the work.

Listen to audio
You learn the names for the parts that break the slowest. But no one teaches you the weight of a pause, the space between a question and the courage to ask it. At the window, evening finds the empty chair. For a moment, we let the light stay.

The work, in voice.
