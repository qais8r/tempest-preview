Berit Lubben

Berit Lubben is a first-year medical student at Mayo Clinic Arizona. Originally from outside Chicago, she attended school in St. Louis and worked in Washington, D.C. during her gap years. She is interested in Emergency Medicine and health policy, particularly addiction medicine. In her free time, she enjoys listening to Supreme Court podcasts, climbing rocks and cliffs, digging through Goodwill bins, and sleeping in a tent and/or hostel bunkbed.
